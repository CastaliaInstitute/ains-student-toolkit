# Ask Gemini Course Setup Questions in Colab

Gemini can help explain how to open a course notebook, understand a setup step, or interpret a basic Python error. It is an optional helper; your course page and instructor remain the source of truth for requirements, deadlines, grading, and submission.

## Use the Colab helper when it is available

1. Open the course notebook in Colab and sign in to the Google account you use for Colab.
2. Look for the **Gemini** button or Gemini side panel in the Colab interface. Google changes its interface and availability over time, and some accounts may not have this feature.
3. Ask one specific question, such as: “I’m in AINS 6001, Module 1. How do I save my own copy of this notebook to Drive? Please give beginner-friendly steps and don’t change my file.”
4. Compare the answer with the course instructions. If it suggests a different tool, account, permission, deadline, or submission method, follow the course instructions and ask the instructor.

## If you do not see Gemini in Colab

Do not add an API key or paste credentials into a notebook to enable a chat panel. You can instead open [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) in another browser tab, if your account provides access, and ask a general setup question. Include only non-sensitive context, such as the course number, module, tool, and step. You can also use the [Colab guide](colab.md) and [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## A useful question template

> I’m taking **[course number]** and working on **[module or activity]** in **[Colab / GitHub / another tool]**. I’m at the step that says **[briefly describe the step]**. Please explain the next setup step in plain language. Do not change files, run code, or guess course policies. If the instructions do not say, tell me what to ask my instructor.

## Keep your work and information safe

- Never share passwords, sign-in codes, API keys, private student information, patient information, or unpublished assessment answers.
- Do not paste an entire private course file into a public AI chat. Ask about the specific setup step instead.
- Review generated code and explanations before using them. AI assistants can be confidently wrong.
- Do not let an assistant submit work, change account permissions, or make course decisions for you.
- Gemini availability and data controls vary by account and organization. Follow your institution's approved-tool and privacy rules.
